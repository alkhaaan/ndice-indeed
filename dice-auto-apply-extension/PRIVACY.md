# Privacy Notice

NDice Indeed stores its settings, logs, and job history locally using Chrome extension storage.

The extension:
- Accesses Dice pages to discover jobs and autofill supported application flows
- Stores profile fields you enter in the popup so they can be reused during automation
- Stores run logs and job history locally in your browser for review
- Stores billing email and license key locally when you choose to use a paid subscription
- Contacts the NDice Indeed subscription service only when you open checkout, open billing management, or refresh subscription status

The extension does not include analytics or sell personal data. Subscription checks are used only to confirm Free, Starter, Pro, or Unlimited plan access.

Use the extension only in accordance with Dice terms, employer expectations, and your local requirements.
